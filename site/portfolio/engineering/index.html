CONTROL LAW / A STARTING POINT

u(t) = Kₚe(t) + K𝒹ė(t)

A proportional–derivative controller reacts both to how far the system is from a target and how quickly that error changes. Working toward safe robot control means understanding what guarantees — and limits — such a law can provide.
